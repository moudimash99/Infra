What the bars mean
  • Downloaded: the video's audio is here, or its text already is (a YouTube transcript needs no download).
  • Transcribed: the video has text, from Whisper on the GPU or from YouTube.
  • Speakers found: who speaks when is known, so each score goes to the right host.
  • Rated: at least one host's score for a game came out of the text.

Every bar counts the same {{ "{:,}".format(t.videos) }} videos. Left out: videos that aren't reviews (news, unboxings, playthroughs){% if t.unavailable %} and {{ "{:,}".format(t.unavailable) }} that YouTube no longer serves{% endif %}.
