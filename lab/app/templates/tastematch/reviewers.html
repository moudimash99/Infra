How the match works

  • For each game, the taste signal is how far a score sits from what BGG's average predicts for that person: everyone loving Brass says little, loving a game BGG shrugs at says a lot.
  • The match is how well your signals and a host's line up on the games you both rated, from −1 (opposite) to +1 (the same). It is pulled toward 0 when you share few games, so a perfect fit on 3 games doesn't beat a good fit on 40.
  • The bar shows the likely range: where 8 in 10 reshuffles of your shared games put the match.
  • Hosts with fewer than 5 games in common with you are listed under “Too few games”, not ranked.
