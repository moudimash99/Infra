How "likely for you" is worked out

It starts from BGG's average, put on {{ "their" if visitor else "your" }} own scale (some people score everything higher or lower than BGG). A game few people have rated counts for less: its average is pulled toward a typical game's, so 300 early backers' 9.0 counts as about 8.5. Then it moves by {{ "their" if visitor else "your" }} taste profile: the kinds of game, designers and mechanics {{ "they rate" if visitor else "you rate" }} above or below that line.

How good is it? Tested in November 2026 on a collection of 143 rated games, hiding one game at a time: it misses the real rating by 0.58 points out of 5 on average, against 0.59 for BGG's average alone. A small gain, but a measured one. Adding the reviewers' scores made it worse (0.60 to 0.72), so they are shown as reasons, not mixed into the number. All scores here are out of 5.
