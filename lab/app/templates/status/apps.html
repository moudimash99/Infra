Two separate rules. Getting in: a pod starts only where its booking fits; a rank that may push others out stops pods of a strictly lower rank on that machine to make room, the others wait. Running out of real memory: ranks don't count; the kernel kills the biggest containers first, and a container above its own limit is killed whatever its rank.
